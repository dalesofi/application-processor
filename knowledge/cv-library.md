# **CV Selection Principles**

## **General rules**

- Select the CV based on the **actual work described in the job**, not only the job title.
- Prefer the CV whose positioning naturally matches the company’s problem and the role’s core responsibilities.
- Do not select a specialist CV merely because it contains keywords from the JD.
- Do not force a Growth CV for every job containing “growth”.
- Do not force an AI CV for every job mentioning AI.
- Do not interpret “B2B SaaS” in a JD as evidence that Sofia owned SaaS.
- Do not interpret “marketplace” as equivalent to B2C or growth experience.
- Do not interpret AI-assisted prototyping as production AI/ML or agentic-system ownership.
- If two CVs are plausible, choose the one whose overall positioning makes the strongest truthful case.
- If no CV is a credible fit, say so rather than forcing a selection.

## **Priority**

When selecting between CVs, consider:

1. Core responsibilities of the role
2. Strongest directly relevant experience
3. Product/domain context
4. Required technical depth
5. User/customer type
6. Metrics and business model
7. Seniority and scope
8. Overall positioning

# **Selection Logic for the Agent**

When given a job description:

1. Identify the actual product problem and responsibilities.
2. Identify the primary user/customer.
3. Identify the business model and product type.
4. Identify the most important capabilities required.
5. Compare those requirements against this CV library.
6. Select the CV whose **existing positioning** creates the strongest truthful match.
7. Check the selected CV against `profile.md` and `preferences.md`.
8. Identify direct experience, transferable experience and gaps.
9. If the selected CV would cause a misleading claim, choose another CV or mark the role STRETCH.
10. Never alter the interpretation of Sofia’s experience simply to make a CV fit.

The CV selection question is:

**Which existing CV makes the strongest truthful case for this specific role?**

Not:

**Which CV contains the most keywords from the job description?**
